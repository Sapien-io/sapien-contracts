# TAM/SAM Analysis: AI Data Quality

This document outlines the Total Addressable Market (TAM) and Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) for two key categories in the AI data quality space: **EOA AI Data Quality** (Human marketplace) and **Agentic Data Quality** (Automation/governance).

---

## 1. EOA AI Data Quality
*Human marketplace, wallet identity*

### TAM (Global)
The TAM can be anchored by two common "labeling" views based on scope:

*   **Data collection & labeling (narrow):** $3.77B (2024) → $4.89B (2025)
*   **Data labeling solution & services (broader):** $18.66B (2024) → $22.46B (2025)
*   **Alternative Benchmark:** $2.61B (2026) → $7.02B (2031) (different scope/definition)

**Realistic EOA TAM range (2025):** ~$5B (narrow) to ~$22B (broad).

### SAM (Addressable Market)
*For an onchain/crypto-native EOA protocol*

Not all labeling spend is realistically "walletized" in the near-term due to procurement, privacy, and enterprise compliance. A practical SAM assumption is based on adoption percentages:

| Scenario | % of TAM | EOA SAM (2025 Range) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conservative** | 2% | $0.10B – $0.45B |
| **Base** | 5% | $0.25B – $1.10B |
| **Aggressive** | 10% | $0.50B – $2.20B |

> *Note: This represents the "wallet-native marketplace" slice that can plausibly move first.*

## 2. Agentic Data Quality
*Automation, agents, governance, observability, data infrastructure*

### TAM (Global)
These categories stack into a significantly larger market:

*   **AI Data Management:** $25.1B (2023) → $70.2B (2028) (CAGR ~22.8%)
    *   *Interpolated 2025 estimate: ~$37.9B*
*   **Data Quality Tools:** $2.78B (2025) → $3.27B (2026) → $7.39B (2031)
*   **Data Observability:** $2.37B (2024) → $4.73B (2030)
*   **AI Governance:** $308.3M (2025) → $3.59B (2033)

**Roll-up Agentic TAM (2025):** **~$43B**

| Category | Estimated 2025 Size |
| :--- | :--- |
| AI Data Management | ~$37.9B |
| Data Quality Tools | ~$2.8B |
| Data Observability | ~$2.4B |
| AI Governance | ~$0.3B |
| **Total** | **~$43.4B** |

### SAM (Addressable Market)
*For an agentic/onchain-verifiable quality layer*

Success depends on penetration into web2 enterprises vs. crypto-native and AI-native builders.

| Scenario | % of TAM | Agentic SAM (2025) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conservative** | 0.5% | ~$215M |
| **Base** | 1.5% | ~$645M |
| **Aggressive** | 3.0% | ~$1.29B |

---

## 3. Comparison Snapshot (2025)

| Category | TAM (Global) | SAM (Addressable) | Key Wedge |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **EOA** | ~$5B – $22B | $0.1B – $2.2B | Human labeling marketplace |
| **Agentic** | ~$43B | $0.2B – $1.3B | Verifiable evals & automated QA |

### Interpretation
*   **EOA** is a cleaner "marketplace wedge" with clearer spend categories (labeling/collection).
*   **Agentic** represents the larger systems budget but is harder to capture quickly without a specific product wedge (e.g., verifiable evals, compliance, or automated QA loops).
